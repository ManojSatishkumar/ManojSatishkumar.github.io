The decision

Why JavaScript?

Until 2019, I was working with various programming languages: C#, Java, and Ruby. Over time, I started thinking:

Why should I learn different programming languages?

In my limited lifetime, if I try to learn everything, I will become a jack of everything and a master of nothing.

So I realized that with Node.js, I can create back-end servers with JS, and with libraries like React, I can create fully-fledged front-end web apps. It made all the sense in the world to master JavaScript.

Lesson #1

Startups are not an easy target

StartupsMakeMyTrip

Big companies have this policy that if you fail the interview, you will not be allowed to give another interview within 6 months. So I started attending interviews with many startups.

My initial experience was very stressful and demotivating. I was asked to implement a Todo app with React & Redux. I wasn't a pro in Redux yet. So I failed. Then I attended an interview at MakeMyTrip. In the 2nd round, I was asked about “async rendering”, and I didn't know about it.

02
Lesson #2

Domain doesn't matter. Skills do.

Another healthcare company

Since Cerner was a healthcare company, I thought if I attend interviews in another healthcare company, I might be given preference.

What I thought mattered
  • Healthcare domain experience
  • A patent
WRONG!
What actually mattered
  • Skills, not experience
  • Answering the JS questions properly

Wrong! It actually didn't matter at all. They wanted skills, not experience. Even when I said I had a patent, it didn't matter if I did not answer the JS interview questions properly.

03
Lesson #3

Don't be in wait mode forever

OracleFeb 2019

In Feb 2019, I attended an interview at Oracle. After a 3.5 hour wait and multiple rounds, I cleared the interview. The director offered me the position. I waited a week, then a month. No signs of any email. One day the HR called and said they found another candidate with “lesser CTC”.

  1. Feb 2019A 3.5 hour wait and multiple rounds
  2. ClearedThe director offered me the position
  3. 1 week laterWaiting…
  4. 1 month laterNo signs of any email
  5. HR calls“We found another candidate with lesser CTC”
The lesson

Don't wait for 1 opportunity even if you cleared all rounds. Keep the options at hand.

04
Lesson #4

Don't be over-confident

IQVIAApr 2019

In April 2019, I gave an interview at IQVIA. Confidence had boosted in me as I had cleared the interview at Oracle. So I went there with the worst weapon possible: over-confidence.

Confidence meter
UnsureConfidentOver-confident

In the 1st round, I encountered a “beast”. He asked the kind of questions that made me feel like I knew nothing about React.js.

Over-confidence won that day. And I lost.

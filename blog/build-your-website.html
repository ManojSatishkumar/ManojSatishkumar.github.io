Let's Build Your Online Portfolio Website

Ten practical steps, from buying your domain to asking for a referral, to help recruiters find you and call you.

Manoj Satishkumar 1 Jan 2024 7 min read 10 steps

If you don't have an online portfolio website yet, now is the time to create one.

Trust me, your portfolio website will play a major role in your career advancement and to bring new opportunities to you. In this world where there are tons of software engineers graduating from colleges, it can become hard for the companies to reach out to the ones who really fit the roles and perform responsibilities.

A personal story

What changed for me

This is my personal experience.

Before
No portfolio website

I did not have an online portfolio website in the starting stages of my career and I did not get many interview calls.

deployed
After
Portfolio live

But after I made it and deployed it, I was surprised at how many HRs thought I will fit a role in their company.

If it has worked for me, it will work for you too.

Key takeaways
  • A portfolio website makes it easier for companies to find you and see that you fit the role.
  • A custom domain, GitHub Pages and HTTPS get it live, and make sure it opens on a recruiter's office laptop.
  • Connect your website, LinkedIn, GitHub README and CV, so each one leads to the next.
  • Put the link at the top of your CV, and don't hesitate to ask for referrals.

05

Enable HTTPS

Don't get blocked

Make sure you enable https:// on your website. This is necessary because the recruiters and interviewers will try to check your portfolio website on their office provided laptops. If your website is missing an SSL certificate, it will be blocked in their VPN.

07

Connect your links, LinkedIn first

Lead the recruiter around

On the homepage of your portfolio, add links to all the social media profiles. LinkedIn should be the first link in that list. The HRs should be encouraged to click on your LinkedIn profile when they visit your website. On the other hand, make sure you add your website link to LinkedIn's featured section.

With these connected links, you will navigate the HR recruiter to different places online and generate curiosity in you and they will feel like giving you a phone call for further discussion. You can always refer to my LinkedIn profile for reference.

Stay active on LinkedIn.
08

Polish your GitHub profile README

For team leads & technical interviewers

Update your GitHub profile README and showcase all your skills over there. This is not for HR but this is for the team leads and technical interviewers because once they receive your profile after initial screening is done by HR, they will most likely click on your GitHub link present in your CV to see what projects you have done.

09

Put your website link at the top of your CV

Make them want to click

Once you are done setting up your portfolio website, please prepare a good looking resume / CV and include your website link on the top. When the HR receives your CV through some communication media, he/she should feel like clicking on your website link. So please highlight it and move the link to the top.

10

Ask for job referrals

Incredibly effective

Don't hesitate to ask your friends and LinkedIn connections for a job referral. It is incredibly effective. Some of the best opportunities I got in my life came through job referrals. This is because, if a company receives your profile through an existing employee, it saves their time in hunting for a fresh candidate and they will have an established trust that you are legit.

Comes through an existing employee Saves their hiring time Established trust

Trust plays a major role in recruitment because as much as you need a job in a good company, the company needs a good candidate like you.

The closing thought

How you present yourself matters

There are a lot of qualified and talented engineers out there but only a few of them make it to the top. One of the distinguishing factors in their career boost is how well they are able to present themselves to the external world.

Think about this

The world's most valuable company, Apple, is incredibly successful because of the way they present their products.

From package unboxing to greeting, everything matters.

We, humans, tend to give our attention to things that are well crafted. We are artists after all. We value creativity and this is what separates us from the rest of the species on this planet. The same rule applies to your portfolio.

Please give extreme attention to detail, describe yourself in a professional way, and make it interesting to read as well.

Trust me, the more time you spend fine-tuning your online portfolio, the more it will be beneficial to you in the long run in your amazing career. I have experienced this happening to me over and over again. So I'm pretty confident this applies universally.

Good luck!

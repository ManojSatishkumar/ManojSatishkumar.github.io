FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Better feed free?

Yes. It's completely free, with no account, subscription or upsell.

Which sites does it work on?

X (Twitter) home timeline, LinkedIn home feed, Reddit home, popular and subreddit feeds, and the Facebook news feed.

Can I still like, comment or reply?

Click any card to open the original post in a new tab, where everything works as usual. Press g anytime to switch back to the normal page.

How do I turn it off on one site?

Use the switches in the toolbar popup, press g on that site, or click the round exit button in the top right of the grid.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or Arc?

Any Chromium-based browser that installs extensions from the Chrome Web Store should work.

Is it made by X, LinkedIn, Reddit or Facebook?

No. Better feed is an independent extension and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of these companies.

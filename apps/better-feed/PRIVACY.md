# Privacy Policy: Better feed

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Better feed does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- The extension runs only on `x.com`, `twitter.com`, `www.linkedin.com`, `www.reddit.com` and `www.facebook.com` pages.
- It reads the posts already displayed on your X home timeline, LinkedIn home feed, Reddit feeds or Facebook news feed and re-arranges them into a grid inside your own browser tab.
- No data leaves your browser. The extension makes no network requests of its own and uses no analytics or third-party services.
- The only things it saves are an on/off preference for each supported site and two display settings (card density and dark mode), stored with Chrome's extension storage (synced across your own signed-in Chrome browsers if Chrome sync is on).

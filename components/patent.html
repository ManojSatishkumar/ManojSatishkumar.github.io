UMR Cloud Network

Unified Medical Record · Health tech

A cloud platform that gives every patient one medical record they own for a lifetime. Built on FHIR standards, it lets specialised healthcare apps plug straight into that record, so nobody has to carry reports, clinical notes and medication history from provider to provider.

  • TodayRecords scattered across every provider you visit
  • With UMROne lifelong record, owned by the patient

Four building blocks
  1. 01

    Patient web portal

    A personalised portal hosting healthcare apps, each carrying the business logic for its own solution.

  2. 02

    Unified records

    No more keeping medical documents, clinical reports and medication history yourself over a lifetime.

  3. 03

    FHIR RESTful APIs

    Standard healthcare APIs let the network and its hosted apps process data and deliver targeted solutions.

  4. 04

    Healthcare app store

    An app store built for FHIR, opening the record to third-party healthcare apps and integrations.
